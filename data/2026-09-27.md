### [1] [Orbital Error Dynamics: Self-Organized Criticality, Ephemeral Parameter Resonance, and Non-Linear Biological Ontologies in Zero-Storage Neural Synthesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30115)
*Volkan Dağlı,Zerrin Dağlı,Dağhan Dağlı*

Main category: cs.NE

TL;DR: 该论文提出"轨道误差动力学"(OED)分析框架,主张突触权重不必以O(W)的静态浮点矩阵形式存储,而可由复二次多项式映射z_{n+1}=z_n^2+c程序化生成、以O(1)的瞬态拓扑共振形式存在,从而规避冯·诺依曼内存瓶颈与表征坍缩。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现代深度神经网络将参数视为存储于物理内存中的静态浮点矩阵,导致冯·诺依曼内存瓶颈和表征坍缩(representation collapse)。作者试图从参数表征的存储范式层面寻找一种非存储式的替代方案,以摆脱内存墙与静态权重的固有局限。

Method: 1) 以复二次多项式映射z_{n+1}=z_n^2+c为内核,将权重参数化为由24字节坐标种子程序化生成的瞬态共振,复杂度从O(W)降至O(1);2) 提出"弯正弦波假说",描述谐振波经环境阻尼向内卷曲至心形尖点c=1/4;3) 定义"观测者地平线几何",给出内部共振肩点X_upper=(0.25,+0.18)与X_lower=(0.25,-0.18),真实边界位于c=0.25±0.50i;4) 引入仿哺乳动物受精"锌火花"的重尾仿生扰动跳跃算子Omega_tunneling以逃离非凸停滞;5) 耦合肠-颅"双脑"架构,并以自适应CD4+调节性免疫门控M_CD4进行屏蔽;6) 将4核苷酸碱基(A、T、C、G)投影到复平面四象限。验证:Two-Moons流形,5个种子,80/20划分,32×32网格,测试期零更新、零标签泄漏,并以模拟光学协处理器作概念性对照。

Result: 程序化参数化在干净测试集上取得77.67%±5.35%准确率,与无约束梯度基线(85.67%±5.35%)的配对差异为8.00个百分点(95% CI:[-1.07%,17.07%]),即差异在统计上不显著;在分布偏移N(1.2,0.4)下为71.33%±3.80%。该参数量化方案与模拟光学协处理器具有概念等价性。

Conclusion: 作者结论为:以24字节坐标种子驱动的程序化参数化可在不存储O(W)权重的前提下,达到与梯度基线统计不可区分的泛化性能,并在分布偏移下保持一定鲁棒性,同时与模拟光学协处理器存在概念等价,提示"参数即过程/共振"的表征范式具备可行性。需注意:样本量小(5种子)、任务仅限二维合成流形,置信区间跨越零点,关于生物机制(锌火花、双脑、CD4门控、核苷酸碱基)的论述属于类比性隐喻,缺乏实证支撑。

Abstract: Modern deep neural networks treat parameters as static floating-point matrices stored in physical memory, incurring Von Neumann memory bottlenecks and representation collapse. We formulate Orbital Error Dynamics (OED), an analytical framework wherein synaptic weights are not stored masses (O(W)), but transient topological resonances (O(1)) derived procedurally from the complex quadratic polynomial map z_{n+1} = z_n^2 + c. We introduce the Bent Sine Wave Hypothesis, demonstrating that non-equilibrium living systems emerge when harmonic waves curl inward through environmental drag toward the cardioid cusp (c = 1/4). We define the Observer Horizon Geometry in parameter space, identifying interior resonance shoulder loci X_upper = (0.25, +0.18) and X_lower = (0.25, -0.18) between the fixed-point basin and the true boundary at c = 0.25 +/- 0.50i. To escape non-convex stagnation without loss zeroing, we introduce a heavy-tailed Biomimetic Perturbed Jump Operator (Omega_tunneling) inspired by mammalian fertilization zinc sparks. We further couple an enteric-cranial Dual-Brain architecture shielded by adaptive CD4+ regulatory immune gating (M_CD4), and project the 4-nucleotide genetic basis (A, T, C, G) across quadrants in C. Multi-seed empirical validation on the Two-Moons manifold (5 seeds, 80/20 train/test split, 32x32 grid, zero test-time updates, zero label leakage) demonstrates that procedural parameterization from a 24-byte coordinate seed achieves 77.67% +/- 5.35% clean test accuracy (within an 8.00-point paired difference of an unconstrained gradient baseline at 85.67% +/- 5.35%, 95% CI: [-1.07%, 17.07%]) and 71.33% +/- 3.80% under distribution shift (N(1.2, 0.4)), alongside conceptual equivalence with an analog optical co-processor.

### [2] [Mitochondria remodel near synapses to boost metabolic capacity and fuel plasticity](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.08.27.672715v3)
*Shah, M.,Ghosh, I.,Shree Ramesh, N.,Pishos, L.,Pancani, T.,Villani, V.,Yasuda, R.,Loomba, S.,Sun, C.,Kamasawa, N.,Rangaraju, V.*

Main category: bioRxiv.Neuroscience

TL;DR: 该研究开发了结合相关光镜与电镜（CLEM）及深度学习分割的流程，从42个2 nm分辨率断层图像中定量分析树突线粒体“近棘区”的三维亚结构，发现近棘区在突触可塑性中结构性增大，并通过增加嵴膜面积、嵴连接、MIC60与ATP合酶等提升局部ATP合成，从而维持而非启动能量反应。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 脑突触是能量消耗热点，学习相关的突触可塑性会急剧增加能量需求；由于大多数突触远离胞体，能量必须局部供给。树突线粒体可在近可塑性棘处快速上调ATP合成，但其“近棘区”（棘基部约2微米片段）是否存在支撑该反应的结构变化尚不清楚，且解析其产能力学结构面临显著技术挑战。

Method: 针对树突线粒体嵴呈复杂、管状、互连网络、需多幅高分辨断层图像才能表征的问题，作者建立了CLEM流程，并配套自定义深度学习模型，在有限训练数据下同时分割五个不同的三维亚细胞器结构，从而在2 nm像素分辨率下对42个断层图像进行定量分析。

Result: 近棘线粒体区域比同一线粒体内的非近棘区域更大，并在突触可塑性过程中进一步增大。近棘线粒体ATP合成与棘ATP水平平行上升；在单棘可塑性中，ATP在数分钟内上升，早于可检测的增大，表明近棘线粒体增大是维持而非启动局部能量反应。增大区域表现为嵴膜表面积、嵴连接数量和高曲率膜面积增加，同时MIC60与ATP合酶拷贝数、内质网接触和更近的核糖体增加。基质靶向PKA抑制降低MIC60磷酸化，并损害近棘线粒体增大、可塑性相关ATP升高及单棘可塑性。

Conclusion: 研究结果支持一个模型：单个线粒体内针对特定棘的重塑可提升局部代谢能力，以维持ATP合成并为突触可塑性供能；其中PKA–MIC60通路是近棘线粒体结构重塑与可塑性相关能量供应的重要环节。

Abstract: Brain synapses are hotspots of energy consumption, and the synaptic plasticity underlying learning sharply increases this demand. Because most synapses lie far from their cell body, their energy demands must be met locally. A dendritic mitochondrion can extend ~30 m and span many spines, yet ramps up ATP synthesis only near spines undergoing plasticity, providing immediate and sustained energy. Whether structural changes in these juxtaspinal mitochondrial regions (2 m segments at spine bases) support this response is unknown; resolving their energy-producing architecture poses substantial challenges. Compared with the lamellar cristae of axonal mitochondria, dendritic mitochondrial cristae form complex, tubular, and interconnected networks that require multiple high-resolution tomograms to characterize. We therefore developed a correlative light and electron microscopy (CLEM) pipeline with a custom deep-learning model that simultaneously segments five distinct 3D suborganellar structures from limited training data, enabling quantitative analysis across 42 tomograms at 2 nm pixel resolution. We find that juxtaspinal mitochondrial regions are larger than non-juxtaspinal regions within the same mitochondrion and enlarge further during synaptic plasticity. Juxtaspinal mitochondrial ATP synthesis and spine ATP levels rise in parallel; during single-spine plasticity, ATP rises within minutes, before detectable enlargement, indicating that juxtaspinal mitochondrial enlargement sustains rather than initiates the local energy response. The enlarged regions exhibit increased cristae membrane surface area, crista junction number, and membrane area with high curvature, together with increased MIC60 and ATP synthase copies, endoplasmic reticulum contacts, and more proximal ribosomes. Matrix-targeted PKA inhibition reduces MIC60 phosphorylation and impairs juxtaspinal mitochondrial enlargement, plasticity-associated ATP increase, and single-spine plasticity. Together, our findings support a model in which spine-specific remodeling within an individual mitochondrion boosts local metabolic capacity to sustain ATP synthesis and fuel synaptic plasticity.

### [3] [Cell Type-Specific Structural and Functional Signatures of APOE4, Age and Sex in the Mouse Anterior Olfactory Nucleus](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.21.753183v1)
*Hu, M.,Kershaw, A.,Brunson, S.,Li, Y.,Zhao, D.,Liu, S.*

Main category: bioRxiv.Neuroscience

TL;DR: 该研究通过电生理记录与形态重建，揭示APOE基因型以细胞特异性方式，通过与年龄和性别的交互作用塑造前嗅核神经元特性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: APOE ε4是晚发型阿尔茨海默病最强的遗传风险因子，可在病理出现前改变神经元生理；但年龄与性别如何在前嗅核（AON）这一早期受累脑区中调节这些效应尚不清楚。

Method: 对成年与老年、雌雄两性的humanized APOE3与APOE4敲入小鼠急性AON脑片中的锥体细胞（PCs）和中间神经元（INs）进行全细胞记录，并结合事后形态学重建，分析结构、内在电生理与突触域的改变。

Result: 在PCs中，形态广泛受APOE基因型×年龄×性别及其交互作用影响。老年E4 PCs的静息膜电位较老年E3更去极化；后超极化幅度呈显著年龄×基因型交互，E4-E3差异方向自成年到老年发生反转。兴奋性突触事件频率呈复杂的基因型、年龄与性别交互，抑制性突触事件幅度在E4小鼠中升高。相比之下，INs改变更为局限：形态变化仅见于树突长度，且仅在E4小鼠中受年龄和性别调节；生理上基因型效应仅限动作电位幅度，而衰老主要改变膜特性和兴奋性突触输入。

Conclusion: APOE基因型通过年龄与性别的交互作用，以细胞特异性方式塑造AON神经元特性，提示APOE4建立了改变的细胞状态，从而影响该环路对后续疾病相关应激的响应。

Abstract: As the strongest genetic risk factor for late-onset Alzheimer's disease (AD), the human APOE {varepsilon}4 allele alters neuronal physiology before overt pathology. However, how aging and sex modify these effects in the anterior olfactory nucleus (AON), an early AD-affected brain region, remains unclear. Here, we performed whole-cell recordings and post hoc morphological reconstructions of pyramidal cells (PCs) and interneurons (INs) in acute AON slices from adult and aged humanized APOE3 (E3) and APOE4 (E4) knock-in mice of both sexes with identification of context-dependent remodeling across structural, intrinsic, and synaptic domains. In PCs, morphology was broadly susceptible to APOE genotype x age x sex and their interactions. Functionally, aged E4 PCs exhibited a more depolarized resting membrane potential than aged E3 PCs. Additionally, afterhyperpolarization amplitude displayed a marked age x genotype interaction, reversing the direction of the E4 - E3 difference from adulthood to aging. Excitatory synaptic event frequency showed a complex genotype and age and sex interaction, whereas inhibitory synaptic event amplitude was elevated in E4 mice. In contrast, INs displayed far more restricted alterations. Morphological changes were limited to dendritic length, which was modulated by age and sex specifically in E4 mice. Physiologically, genotype effects were confined to action potential amplitude, while aging primarily altered membrane properties and excitatory synaptic inputs. Together, these findings demonstrate that APOE genotype cell-specifically shapes AON neuronal properties through interactions with age and sex, suggesting that APOE4 establishes altered cellular states that influence how this circuit responds to subsequent disease-related stress.
